# BiO-Slotz Slot Rules

Your cabinet art stays untouched. The **game logic** is now real: paytable + paylines + Wild + Scatter + Free Spins.

## Layout
- **Reels:** 5
- **Rows:** 3
- **Paylines:** 3 (Top, Middle, Bottom)
- **Wins pay left→right** starting from Reel 1.

## Betting
- **BET button** cycles **bet per line**.
- **Total Bet = betPerLine × 3 lines**.
- Paid spins subtract Total Bet from Credits.
- Free spins do **not** subtract Credits.

## Special Symbols
- **WILD**: `s22` Alien (substitutes for any non-scatter symbol on paylines).
- **SCATTER**: `s12` Mystery `?` (pays anywhere, awards Free Spins).

## Scatter (s12) awards

| Scatters anywhere | Payout | Free Spins |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 2× Total Bet | +5 |
| 4 | 10× Total Bet | +10 |
| 5 | 50× Total Bet | +20 |

## Examples
- **Diamond ×5 on any line:** 1000 units × betPerLine
- **Crown ×5:** 750 units × betPerLine
- **Money Bag ×4:** 120 units × betPerLine
- **Any 3-of-kind** on Top/Middle/Bottom line pays its 3-of-kind value.
